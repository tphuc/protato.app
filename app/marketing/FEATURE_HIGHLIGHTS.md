# Protato - Feature Highlights for Marketing

Key differentiators and user-facing features for landing page, App Store, and promotional materials.

---

## 🎯 Core Value Proposition

> **Create professional 3D device mockups with animation in minutes, not hours.**

Protato combines a 3D scene editor, timeline animator, and video exporter in one native Mac/iOS app.

---

## 🏆 Top 10 Features

### 1. **22 Real Device Models**
- iPhone 17 series (7 colors), iPhone 17 Air (4), iPhone 15 Pro Max (4)
- iPad A16, MacBook Pro 14
- Pixel-perfect dimensions, correct corner radii, pivot-corrected rotation

### 2. **Native 60fps Animation**
- RealityKit-powered playback (not SwiftUI animation)
- Multiple devices perfectly synchronized
- Scrub timeline without dropping frames

### 3. **Block-Based Timeline Editing**
- Intuitive "motion blocks" instead of abstract keyframes
- Recording zones: gesture edits the motion you're over
- Auto-split blocks on capture
- Base transform gaps for resting poses

### 4. **Screen Content Playback**
- Videos & images on device screens
- Frame-accurate sync with device motion
- Trim, loop, fit modes (.fill/.fit/.stretch)
- Multiple clips per device

### 5. **Professional Lighting**
- 4-point studio rig (key, fill, rim, back)
- 9 IBL skybox environments
- 3 one-click modes: Studio / Dramatic / Flat
- Per-light intensity + exposure control

### 6. **Versatile Backgrounds**
- Solid, gradient, image, mesh gradient, transparent
- 12 animated mesh gradient presets
- Interactive mesh editor
- Export with transparency (images)

### 7. **Composition Overlays**
- Text, images, shapes, stickers
- Per-property keyframes (position, scale, rotation, opacity)
- Depth layers: behind or in front of 3D devices
- Text character animation
- Blend modes (multiply, screen, overlay)

### 8. **Broadcast-Quality Export**
- HEVC (H.265) at 720p/1080p/1440p
- 24/30/60 fps
- Frame-accurate baking (zero timing drift)
- Metal GPU compositing
- Cancelable with progress

### 9. **Native Mac & iOS**
- Single purchase, runs on both
- iCloud project sync
- Touch-optimized on iPad/iPhone
- Keyboard/mouse/trackpad on Mac

### 10. **Generous Free Tier**
- 20 free exports on sign-in
- 3 devices per scene
- Core features unlocked
- No ads, no watermarks
