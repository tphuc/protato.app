## Usage Guidelines for Marketing

### Do ✅
- Show multiple colors in hero images
- Demonstrate color-swapping feature
- Use correct marketing names
- Show realistic proportions (devices to scale)
- Include MacBook for "desktop + mobile" workflows

### Don't ❌
- Modify device colors beyond provided variants
- Stretch/distort device proportions
- Show unreleased/unannounced models
- Use competitor device imagery
- Remove Dynamic Island / notch from renders
