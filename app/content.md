### Key Strategic Insight

The **biggest gap** in the entire competitive landscape is **outcome-based guides** (Cluster 3). Every competitor writes "how to make app store screenshots" but none write "how to make mockups for Product Hunt" or "how to showcase your app on Dribbble." These have:

- **Lower competition** (sites rank for the generic term, not the specific use case)
- **Higher conversion** (someone searching for Product Hunt mockups is actively launching)
- **Better backlink potential** (Product Hunt blogs, startup newsletters)
- **Clearer search intent** (the user knows exactly what they want)

This is where Protato should invest first for SEO differentiation.
