Protato is a 3D iPhone mockup generator and app screenshot maker for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Create realistic device mockups, animated app previews, product videos, and marketing visuals directly on your Apple devices.

Import screenshots or screen recordings, customize 3D devices, lighting, backgrounds, camera angles, and animations, then export high-resolution images or 4K videos. Protato is useful for App Store screenshot design, app marketing, social media content, product demos, advertisements, launch videos, and presentations.

Built for indie developers, UI/UX designers, marketers, and founders, Protato makes professional 3D app mockups accessible without Blender or other complex 3D tools.
